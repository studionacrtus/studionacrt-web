Planning A Condo Renovation In Miami

Renovating A Condo Or Penthouse In A High Rise Is Different From Renovating A House. The Design Opportunities Are Real, But So Are The Building's Constraints. Thinking About Scope Early, Before You Fall In Love With A Specific Finish, Makes The Whole Project Calmer. Here Is How We Suggest Approaching It.

Start With How You Want To Live, Not With A Shopping List

The Most Useful First Step Is Describing How The Home Should Work: Where You Cook And Gather, Where You Want Quiet, How You Use The View. Scope Decisions Become Much Easier When They Trace Back To A Clear Intent Rather Than A Collection Of Individual Wants.

Separate What Is Cosmetic From What Is Structural

Some Changes Are Surface Level, Finishes, Joinery, Lighting, Furniture. Others Touch Layout, Plumbing Routes, Or How Rooms Connect. It Is Worth Knowing Which Category Each Idea Falls Into Early, Because The Second Group Carries More Coordination And Usually More Time. A Good Design Brief Sorts These Clearly.

Understand The Building's Realities Early

Towers Have Their Own Logic: Sound Between Units, Structural Elements That Cannot Move, Shared Systems, And Rules About How And When Work Can Happen. We Design With These In Mind From The Start And Prepare Documentation That Can Be Reviewed Against A Building's Requirements, Rather Than Discovering Limits Partway Through.

  • How Sound Travels Between Units And Floors
  • Which Walls And Elements Are Structural
  • Where Plumbing And Mechanical Routes Can And Cannot Go
  • Building Rules On Work Hours, Access, And Approvals

Decide The Design Before You Commit To The Build

The Most Expensive Changes Are The Ones Made After Construction Starts. We Resolve The Design And Visualize It Photoreal First, So Choices About Light, Material, And Layout Are Settled Before Anyone Is On Site. That Is Where Visualization Removes Risk.

Keep One Team From Concept Through Execution

Handing A Project Between Disconnected Parties Is Where Intent Gets Lost. Keeping Design, Documentation, And Ongoing Design Review Under One Team Means The Person Who Set The Vision Is The Same One Checking The Result. It Is The Approach We Take On Every Project.
