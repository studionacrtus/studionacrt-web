How Our Luxury Condo Interior Design Process Works

A high-rise condo or penthouse is a home inside a larger building, and the design process reflects that. Below is how we approach a project at Studio NACRT US — a clear sequence of stages, each ending with something you can review and approve before the next begins.

1. First conversation and brief

Everything starts with understanding how you want to live in the space: the rhythm of your days, who uses which rooms, and the feeling you want when you walk in. We look at the unit's orientation, the views, and how daylight moves across the floor through the day. This becomes a written brief that guides every later decision.

2. Concept and design language

Next we develop a single, coherent design language for the whole home rather than decorating room by room. We work in stone, wood, concrete, glass, and light, and we test how those materials read against your particular views and daylight. You see the direction early, as a considered concept rather than a catalogue of options.

3. Space planning and detailing

With the concept agreed, we plan the space in detail: layouts tuned to light and sightlines, millwork and integrated storage, and the lighting scheme that shifts a room from morning to evening. In a tower, this stage also accounts for the practical realities of the building — sound, structure, and shared systems — so the design is buildable, not just beautiful.

4. Photoreal visualization

Before anything is ordered or built, we visualize the space photoreal. Seeing accurate light, true materials, and the real spatial feel of each room lets you make confident decisions and catch things that are hard to judge from samples alone. It is the point where the design stops being abstract.

5. Documentation and specification

We prepare the drawings and specifications the build needs, and organize them so they can be reviewed against a building's requirements where that applies. Clear documentation is what keeps the built result faithful to what you approved.

6. On-site oversight through install

We stay involved during construction, coordinating with your build team and reviewing the work on site through final install. The goal is simple: the room you approved on screen is the room you get.
