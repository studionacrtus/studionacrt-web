Luxury Condo & Penthouse Interiors

High-rise living has its own logic. We design condo and penthouse interiors around the light, the views, and the practical realities of the building — then carry one material-led design language through every room.

A condo or penthouse is a home inside a larger system. The best results come from designing with the building in mind: how daylight moves across the floorplate through the day, how the view frames each room, and how sound, structure, and shared services shape what is possible. We plan around those constraints instead of fighting them.

Our approach favors stone, wood, concrete, glass, and considered lighting over decoration. We develop a single, coherent design language and detail it precisely — from millwork and integrated storage to the lighting that shifts a room from morning to evening. Every project is visualized photoreal before anything is built, so you can make confident decisions on layout, materials, and views.

What's Included

  • Full concept and design development
  • Space planning tuned to light and views
  • Millwork, joinery, and integrated storage detailing
  • Material, finish, and lighting specification
  • Documentation prepared for building review
  • Photoreal 3D visualization before build

Condo & Penthouse FAQ

Do you design condo and penthouse interiors in Miami?

Yes. Studio NACRT US designs and renovates luxury condo and penthouse interiors across Miami and South Florida, including Miami Beach, Brickell, Sunny Isles, Bal Harbour, and Fort Lauderdale high-rises.

Can you work within a building's renovation rules?

Our design process accounts for the practical realities of tower living, including sound, structure, and shared systems. We coordinate our drawings and specifications so they can be reviewed against a building's requirements before work begins.

Do you provide 3D visualization for condo projects?

Yes. Every condo and penthouse project includes photoreal 3D visualization, so you can judge light, views, materials, and layout before committing to a build.
