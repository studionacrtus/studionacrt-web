Awarded Residential Design Project

Coconut Grove Residence

An Awarded Residential Interior Design Project, In Development. Studio NACRT US Is Developing The Interior Design, Visualization, Documentation, And Furnishings For A Waterfront Residence In Coconut Grove, Florida.

Residences · Coconut Grove, Florida

Coconut Grove Residence Is An Awarded Project Studio NACRT US Is Developing For A Waterfront Residence In Coconut Grove, Florida. The Work Spans Interior Design, Photoreal 3D Visualization, Documentation, And Furnishings, Resolved Together As One Coherent Design Language Before Execution.

The Interiors Favor Stone, Wood, Concrete, Glass, And Light Over Decoration For Its Own Sake. Each Room Is Studied In Photoreal 3D So Material, Layout, And Lighting Decisions Can Be Confirmed Early, And So The Built Result Matches What Is Designed And Approved. Studio NACRT US Coordinates Execution Through Completion.

Scope

  • Full Interior Design And Design Development
  • Material, Finish, And Lighting Specification
  • Custom Furniture And Furnishings
  • Photoreal 3D Visualization Of Every Room
  • Documentation For Execution
  • Execution Coordination Through Completion
