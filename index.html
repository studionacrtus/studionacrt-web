Frequently Asked Questions

What does Studio NACRT US do?

Studio NACRT US is a luxury interior design firm serving Miami and South Florida. We design residential, condo and penthouse, multifamily, hospitality, and commercial interiors, and produce photoreal 3D visualization for every project.

Where does NACRT US work?

We focus on Miami and South Florida, including Miami Beach, Brickell, Coral Gables, Coconut Grove, Bal Harbour, Sunny Isles, Fort Lauderdale, and Palm Beach. We also take on selected work beyond the region.

How is NACRT US related to Studio NACRT in Belgrade?

NACRT US is the U.S. branch of Studio NACRT, an interior design studio founded in Belgrade in 2014. The same design team supports U.S. projects, so American clients work with an established studio rather than a brand-new practice.

What makes NACRT's approach different?

Material-led minimalism. We build interiors from stone, wood, concrete, glass, and light rather than decoration, keep one team involved from concept through supervision, and visualize every project photoreal before it is built.

How do I start a project?

Email info@studionacrt.us or use our contact form with your space, location, and timeline. We respond to every serious inquiry.
