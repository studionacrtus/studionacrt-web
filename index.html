Frequently Asked Questions

What Does Studio NACRT US Do?

Studio NACRT US Is A Luxury Interior Design Firm That Is Miami Based And Serves Clients Across The United States. We Design Residential, Condo And Penthouse, Multifamily, Hospitality, And Commercial Interiors, And Produce Photoreal 3D Visualization For Every Project.

Where Does Studio NACRT US Work?

Studio NACRT US Is Miami Based And Serves Clients Across The United States. We Work Throughout The Miami Area, Including Miami Beach, Brickell, Coral Gables, Coconut Grove, Bal Harbour, Sunny Isles, Fort Lauderdale, And Palm Beach, And Take On Selected Projects Nationwide.

What Makes Studio NACRT US Different?

Studio NACRT US Designs Interiors Around Stone, Wood, Concrete, Glass, And Light Rather Than Decoration, Keeps One Team Involved From Concept Through Design Review, And Visualizes Every Project In Photoreal 3D Before Execution Begins.

How Do I Start A Project?

Email info@studionacrt.us Or Use Our Contact Form With Your Space, Location, And Timeline. We Respond To Every Serious Inquiry.
